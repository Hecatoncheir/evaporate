Redesign the icon as a compact rounded-square warm-white hardware faceplate.

near-black circular recessed grille. Add one vivid orange-red status light.
Keep the symbol centered, front-facing, tactile, and readable at 16–32 px.

Palette: warm bone white, cool aluminium gray, charcoal, orange-red, with only
a tiny ice-blue reflection. No letters, words, numbers, copied brand marks,
cables, photographic background, watermark, presentation frame, or perspective
